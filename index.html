Our Mission

Help immigrant-owned businesses grow.

We bring visibility and support to immigrant-owned businesses by creating a connected community of businesses that everyone can support. The focus is measurable outcomes—more customers, stronger revenue, and sustained visibility across that network.

Why I started Pathway

When I was in middle school, my friends and I spent hours at a small local restaurant. It was our spot — warm, welcoming, full of community. We felt at home there.

As we grew older, that place quietly shut down. Not because the food was bad. Not because the owner didn't care. It closed because it lacked visibility and support. No one knew it was there.

That loss inspired Pathway. No business should disappear simply because people can't find it.

Our values

Frequently Asked Questions

How is this free?
We're currently offering completely free services to the first 20 immigrant-owned businesses as part of our launch initiative. There are no hidden fees or future charges — you keep everything forever.
Do I own my website and social accounts?
Yes! You get full administrative access to everything we create — your Google Business Profile, website, Instagram, TikTok, and all analytics. You can change anything anytime.
What if I want to stop working with Pathway?
You can leave anytime — no contracts, no hard feelings. Everything we built stays yours forever. We'd love to have you back if you ever need support again.
What types of businesses do you help?
We specialize in immigrant-owned family businesses: restaurants, salons, daycare centers, retail shops, and service providers. If you have a physical location or local service, we can help you get more customers.
How long does the process take?
Most businesses see results within 2-3 weeks. Website launch takes 7-10 days, and social media growth starts immediately. We work at your pace.
