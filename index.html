About

Why Pathway exists

When I was in middle school, my friends and I spent hours at a small local restaurant. It was our spot: warm, welcoming, full of people we knew.

As we got older, it quietly closed. Not because the food was bad, and not because the owner did not care. It closed because nobody could find it.

That is why Pathway exists. No business should disappear simply because people cannot find it.
